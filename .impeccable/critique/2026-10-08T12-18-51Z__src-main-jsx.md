## Overall Impression
Every section is shaped and pitched the same, so there is no rhythm and no peak after the hero; mood darkens toward the end (navy scrims, overcast About, navy contact, black footer) against the "sunlit" brief; hero asks for tickets three times and never for a quote; visible bugs (route board, footer caps) undercut trust.
