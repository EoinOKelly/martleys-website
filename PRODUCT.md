# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in and around Portlaoise arranging reliable group, school, event, and private transport. They arrive needing quick reassurance and a clear route to an enquiry or timetable.

## Product Purpose

Present Martley's as the confident, welcoming local coach partner for every scale of journey, and make it effortless to request a quote or find a school service.

## Brand Personality

Sunlit, capable, neighbourly. The site should feel like a clean coach on a bright Irish morning: professional without corporate distance.

## Anti-references

Avoid dark, rainy-feeling overlays, heavy blocks of colour, orange accents, generic travel-booking interfaces, and stock-template service cards.

## Design Principles

- Put the journey in motion, without making the interface busy.
- Give practical information an immediate, reassuring home.
- Let local experience feel personal and polished.
- Use colour as signposting, not as weight.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Design for WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable type, responsive layouts, and a reduced-motion experience. Make accessible travel visibly part of the offer.
